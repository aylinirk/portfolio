blocks:
  - label: Brief
    heading: The brief
    paras:
      - "The Spielgruppe Seon needed a website. Not just a page, but an online presence that parents could trust and find easily. The goal: give families the information they need to decide if the group is right for their child, and make it easy to get in touch."
      - There was no existing site, no brand guidelines, and no template to start from. Just a need and a deadline.
  - label: Problem
    heading: The problem
    paras:
      - Parents looking for a local playgroup rely heavily on first impressions. A missing or poorly structured website looks disorganised. And for a group working with young children, trust is everything.
      - The challenge was to create a site that felt warm and welcoming without being unprofessional, and that answered the most important questions (What happens there? When? For whom? How do I register?) without overwhelming new visitors.
insightQuote: For parents of young children, trust is the product. The website either builds it in the first ten seconds or loses it.
insightAttr: Core insight
prinHeading: Design priorities
principles:
  - number: "1"
    title: Answer the key questions immediately
    body: Parents scan, they do not read. Age range, schedule, location and registration had to be visible without scrolling.
  - number: "2"
    title: Warm, not childish
    body: The visual tone had to appeal to parents, not children. Trustworthy, friendly and clean rather than busy or cartoon-like.
  - number: "3"
    title: Low maintenance for the team
    body: The group is run by volunteers. The site had to be simple enough that non-technical people could update it without my help.
procHeading: How it came together
process:
  - number: "01"
    title: Understand the real audience
    body: The users were not the playgroup staff, but parents, most of them new to the area or new to the concept of a Spielgruppe. I started by mapping out what they would want to know and in what order.
  - number: "02"
    title: Define the information architecture
    body: "I structured the content around the key questions parents have: what the group offers, who it is for, when and where it meets, and how to register. Simple, linear, no dead ends."
  - number: "03"
    title: Design and build
    body: "I designed the layout first, then built the site. The visual language stayed clean and warm: readable typography, soft colours and a clear hierarchy. That way, the content comes first."

pull: A small project with real stakes is still a *real* project.
reflection:
  - Spielgruppe Seon was my first end-to-end design and build project for a real client with real users. It taught me that even a simple brief requires real research. The users’ needs and the client’s needs are rarely the same thing.
  - It also confirmed that constraints (no budget, no brand, no existing content) are not obstacles. They are the brief.
