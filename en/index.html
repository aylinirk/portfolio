About

Curious, creative and sometimes a little too focused on details.

My journey into design started with the Google UX Design Course on Coursera. What began with my first wireframes and prototypes soon turned into my own projects. At some point, I knew: I wanted to learn more!

Since August 2026, I’ve been studying Interaction Design HF at the School of Design in Zurich. From web technologies and game design to design thinking, there’s a lot to explore. I especially enjoy bringing my own ideas to life, trying new things and sometimes getting a little frustrated when the code doesn’t do what I want it to.

I have experience in user research, prototyping, usability testing and designing digital interfaces. I enjoy working with Figma, FigJam, AI tools and, more recently, HTML, CSS and JavaScript.

I’m a direct person, a quick learner and someone who likes to ask: Could this be done in a simpler way? My experience in process improvement helps me stay organised and see the bigger picture. Even if I sometimes spend a little too much time on one tiny design detail.

At the end of the day, I want to create designs that not only look good but actually work for the people using them.

First make it functional, then fast, and then, pretty.
